QR CODE:
The included QR code currently points to https://crownsstationery.com/.
Before printing it on real books, replace that destination with your actual published website domain and regenerate the QR code. A QR code cannot automatically know a domain you have not chosen yet.

PUBLISH:
Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js and the assets folder together to your web host.
